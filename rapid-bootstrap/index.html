Welcome To Legends of Tal Dorei!

Legends of Tal Dorei is the name of the D&D campaign I run on Sundays for a group of like-minded nerdy friends. It started in April of 2023 and gone from levels 1 to 11 currently. This is the story of a scrappy and unlikely party called The Mistfits as they try to save the continent of Tal Dorei; and the World for that matter, from the evil machinations of the Lich King Neminar Dressig!

Discover Adventures in the world of Tal Dorei!

The Campaign

This Dungeons & Dragons campaign is my own homebrew adventure set on the continent of Tal Dorei in the world of Exandria, the world created and used by the hit D&D actual play; and now animated tv show, Critical Role. However, this story takes place roughly 23 years after the events of the first campaign and show Vox Machina. We play every Sunday, or at least we sure try. The cities and kingdoms of Tal Dorei have rebuilt over the years since the brutal war with the Chroma Conclave; an alliance of powerful and malicious Ancient Dragons. Unfortunately, nothing lasts forever, not even peace. Whispers of strange happenings begin occurring across the land. Monster attacks become increasingly frequent, the wilderness gangs of The Ravagers grow bolder and begin attacking towns and cities, a new Cult of The Black Hand arises. On top of the countless troubles that seem to be blooming around every corner a new magic drug called Null seems to be fueling much of the chaos as cities only recently rebuilt struggle to face the growing terrors outside and inside their gates. At the center of it all a single name arises, The Quiet King. Eventually revealing themselves to be none other than Neminar Dressig; a cruel wizard and tyrant thought to have died over 300 years ago. He has now risen a Lich and set his foul minions against the innocents of Tal Dorei in his search for the Luxon Beacon. Should he find the Beacon he would gain the power over chance and time and become greater than even the Gods. Our adventurers, The Mistfits, journey across Tal Dorei in their quest to stop Neminar. The Lich Neminar is powered by the Betrayer God Bane but should the party find the Beacon first they have a chance. With hope and faith in their companions the Misfits battle against the forces of evil to save Tal Dorei, and the World!

Meet the Characters!

Sam

Fighter/Ranger Holding the rank of Expositor of the Cobalt Soul Sam has continuously sought out justice where he can. Cobalt Monks are truth seekers by nature and almost nothing escapes Sam’s vigilance as the sentry of the Mistfits. But you can’t live off justice, and so Sam has developed a appreciation and skills for nature and cooking, much to the pleasure of his full-bellied companions. A follower of the Goddess Wildmother Sam now wields the Vestige sword the Danu Vine Blade. He’s learned to become a serious threat to enemy mages but there’s one final spell caster his green blade seeks!

Newt

Paladin The son of the Verdant Lord Celindar; the commander of the elven city of Syngorn’s military, Newt was raised to martial prowess from a early age. A child of prophecy marked by his odd appearance compared to the elves of Syngorn he was foretold to be the savior of the city. Chaffing under the strict rules of Syngorn and life as a “Chosen One” Newt left his home to seek a life for himself. Becoming a devotee of Avandra The Chane Bringer, he has learned to be less burdened. He now wields the dark Vestige Ruin’s Wake. However fate has its funny ways as he finds himself the newest member of the Misfits, joining their quest to stop Neminar and prevent disaster. Should he succeed irony will have seen him leave his city in order to find the means to save it as was seen long ago!

Preston

Bard/Barbarian A denizen of the Feywild; a realm of magic and high emotion, Preston always had a tune in his heart and a itch in his step. Eventually crossing into the world of Tal Dorei he sought out new adventure and new sights. Unlucky for him he arrived during a time of turmoil, though lucky for him he managed to meet and join the Mistfits. Travelling with such a group has brought him countless adventures and new experiences. Finding new strengths in himself he now aids his companions in their heroic struggle. Even reaching such heights as becoming the new Interplanar Wrestling Champion!

Quinn

Arcane Trickster Rogue Quinn grew up in a small town with big ambitions. She eventually left her quiet life to see the world, but the world can be a…complicated. Quinn found herself a member of the Clasp; the thieves guild that operates across Tal Dorei. At one point a job went sideways and she found herself on the run with a sizeable bounty on her head. Through some very risky deal making and bloody struggles Quinn and the Mistfits managed to clear her marker. Holding the shadowy Vestige The Infiltrator’s Key she’s now free to help her companions fight a God powered evil undead wizard!

Orion

Once a member of the Krynn Dynasty; a kingdom of Drow across the sea, Orion escaped just ahead of the hangman’s noose. His people worship the otherworldly Luxon Beacon, and it seemed to reject him, marking him an enemy of Krynn. Orion a talented mage and seer has been able to divine the Beacon exiled him to seek its counterpart on the continent of Tal Dorei. Now possessing the Vestige mage tome Index Calleum he combines his mastery of the arcane with his companions to find the emerging Beacon and stop Neminar from corrupting it!

Neminar Dressig

Lich Tal Dorei wasn’t always called such, some 300 years ago during the events of the Scattered War a part of it was called Dressig and its king was Neimar Dressig. Inheriting his crown and war from his cruel father Warren Dressig, Neminar would be a different type of king. Not brutish, but cunning and skilled in the ways of the arcane. Reaching skills beyond any other living mage he eventually even received tutelage from the Betrayer God Bane. Eventually Neminar was defeated, but death was just a delay to the chosen disciple of the Dark Lord of Tyranny. Rising a lich; a undead wizard, he set about his evil machinations. Setting off numerous catastrophes across the continent so no one could interfere in his true goal. Find the Luxon Beacon and use its power to bend the world to his iron will. Now only the Mistfits stand against the madness of Neminar Black Hand!

The Adventure so far...

Drynna & The Mist

The adventure begins in the usually quiet town of Dryanna. Sitting by the enormous lake Mooren this fishing village has been having trouble with a strange mist and the dangerous that hides in it when the party arrives. After investigating and being aided by the Archdruid Arcius the party discovers the mist’s origin. A coven of hags; the Cutter Sisters, has been freed from their bindings and has begun using the mist and creatures of the Shadowfell to try to overrun the village. The party eventually travels across lake Mooren to its central island and defeat the hags, freeing the town of their curse. They learn the Cutter Sister’s were freed by and obey a entity named The Quiet King.

Westrunn & Null

Having saved the town of Dryanna the newly crowned Mistfits continue their travels to the city of Westrunn. Once arrived they find it plagued by Null; a magic drug that drives people mad. They also hear about the numerous catastrophic events occurring across the continent and again the Quiet King is mentioned. After encounters in the nearby Bramblewood and even the sewers of Westrunn the party manages stop the Null trade in the city. They even expose a crooked cop and defeat a deadly assassin, and claim his Vestige the Infiltrator’s Key. They finally discover the identity of the Quiet King to be Neminar Dressig, thought to be dead some 300 years.

The Tower of Wrettis

The Mistfits travel to the Grey Witch Circia looking for a cure for Sam’s mentor Greystone. Upon successfully helping the witch, Circia agrees to cure Greystone, and even tells them she once knew Neminar. She provides what information she can on her old adventuring companion and sets them to seek more in the mysterious Tower of Wrettis; a forbidden wizard’s observatory. On the way to the tower they meet Newt. Once at the tower they quickly discover why it was forbidden, as the madness and chaos that overtook the wizard who created it threatens to overwhelm the party. The Mistfits manage to prevail and learn of powerful artifacts; Vestiges of Divergence, that should they seek out could give them the advantage they need to defeat Neminar.

The Search for Vestiges

Quickly in their search they run afoul of Neminar himself, as he twists the defensive magics of the elven city of Syngorn. The party survives the encounter and continues their search for the Vestiges, and a way to help Syngorn. They battle undead and Neminar’s former mentor, travel the depths of the Underdark and face eldritch powers, battle giants and elemental storms, and throw a kick ass wrestling event in the Feywild. The Mistfits are now armed with the powerful Vestiges of Divergence, but are they truly ready to face the Lich Neminar Black Hand?
